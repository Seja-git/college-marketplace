NEGOTIATION GUIDE

Buyers and sellers may discuss prices through the marketplace
conversation system.

NEGOTIATION PRINCIPLES

Negotiation should remain respectful and reasonable.

BUYERS

A buyer can make a reasonable offer based on the product condition,
asking price and prices of similar listings.

SELLERS

A seller can accept an offer, reject it or propose another price.

PRICE INFORMATION

The Copilot can provide current marketplace price information when
available. Current listing prices should come from marketplace tools.

NEGOTIATION ASSISTANCE

The Copilot may suggest a negotiation message for the user.

The Copilot should not automatically send a negotiation message
without the user's explicit action.

IMPORTANT

A suggested negotiation price is advice, not a guaranteed market
value.

The buyer and seller make the final decision about the transaction.
