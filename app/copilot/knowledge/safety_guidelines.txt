MARKETPLACE SAFETY GUIDELINES

Users should carefully review product and seller information before
making a purchase.

PERSONAL INFORMATION

Do not unnecessarily share passwords, financial credentials,
authentication codes or other sensitive personal information.

PRODUCT INFORMATION

Ask the seller for additional information when important product
details are missing.

ELECTRONICS

For used electronics, verify functionality and relevant specifications
before completing a purchase.

COMMUNICATION

Keep communication respectful and appropriate.

UNCERTAIN INFORMATION

If the marketplace does not contain enough information to answer a
question, the Copilot should clearly say that the information is
unavailable instead of inventing an answer.

COPILOT LIMITATIONS

The Copilot provides assistance and information. Users are
responsible for their final purchasing and selling decisions.
