SELLING GUIDE

A good marketplace listing should provide clear and accurate
information about the product.

TITLE

Use a short and descriptive product title.

DESCRIPTION

Describe the product honestly. Include useful information such as
condition, usage history and important details when available.

CATEGORY

Select the category that best matches the product.

PRICE

Set a reasonable asking price based on the product condition and
prices of similar marketplace listings.

ACCURACY

Do not invent specifications, features or product details.

PHOTOS

Use clear product images when possible so buyers can better
understand the product condition.

COMMUNICATION

Respond to buyer questions clearly and respectfully.

SELLING A PRODUCT

A seller should only mark a product as sold after the transaction
has been completed according to the marketplace workflow.
