COLLEGE MARKETPLACE FAQ

The College Marketplace is a platform for students to buy and sell
second-hand products within the college community.

PRODUCT CATEGORIES

Marketplace products can belong to categories such as:
Books, Electronics, Notes, Cycles, Furniture, Sports,
Hostel Essentials, Stationery, Tools and Others.

PRODUCT AVAILABILITY

A listing is available when it has not been marked as sold.
The Copilot should use the live marketplace database to verify
whether a specific product is currently available.

PRODUCT PRICES

Product prices shown by the Copilot should come from current
marketplace listings when discussing actual products.

The marketplace uses Indian Rupees (₹).

SELLER INFORMATION

Users can view seller information and marketplace ratings.
Seller ratings should be based on actual reviews stored in the
marketplace database.

WISHLIST

Users can add available marketplace products to their wishlist.
A product that has already been sold cannot be added to a wishlist.

SEARCH

Users can search for products using product names, descriptions,
categories and price constraints.

CONTACTING SELLERS

Buyers can contact sellers through the marketplace conversation
system to ask questions or discuss a product.

REVIEWS

Users can review sellers after eligible marketplace interactions.
Reviews contain a rating and optional comment.
