BUYING GUIDE

Before purchasing a second-hand product, buyers should inspect
the available product information carefully.

PRODUCT CONDITION

Read the product description carefully and check any information
provided by the seller about condition, usage and visible issues.

PRICE

Compare similar marketplace listings before deciding whether the
asking price is reasonable.

SELLER

Review the available seller information and seller ratings.
Seller ratings should be treated as historical marketplace
feedback and should not be considered a guarantee of future behavior.

QUESTIONS

If important information is missing from the listing, buyers can
contact the seller through the marketplace conversation system.

ELECTRONICS

For electronics, buyers should verify relevant information such as
model, compatibility, functionality and visible condition when that
information is available.

BOOKS AND NOTES

For books and notes, buyers should check the title, edition or
content information when available.

FINAL DECISION

The buyer should make the final purchasing decision based on the
available product information, seller information and personal
requirements.
